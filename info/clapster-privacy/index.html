Clapster Privacy Policy

Clapster requires access to your device's microphone to record your clap backs in order to evaluate their correctness and accuracy. Evaluation is performed on your device and therefore the recordings never leave your device; they are deleted immediately after the evaluation has been completed. Recordings made by Clapster will always belong to you and are never shared with others.

Clapster respects your privacy and you can rest assured that we take it seriously.
