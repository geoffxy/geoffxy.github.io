Wow, it's been more than half a year since my last blog post!

Quite a bit has changed since my last post. I'm currently a Software Engineering student at the [University of Waterloo](https://uwaterloo.ca/), and nearly finished first year. Things have been very hectic but I can definitely say that I've learned so much in these past seven months---both academically and non-academically. But I'll save that for a future blog post.

<!-- readmore -->

I'm also glad to say that I've made the switch from Tumblr to [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/) as my blogging platform. While Tumblr worked well, it included a lot of features that I didn't specifically need. Running my blog off Jekyll gives me a lot more control over how everything is displayed and it also makes writing posts a lot easier.

Keep your eye out for more posts to come. Hopefully I'll be blogging a lot more frequently once this term is over.
