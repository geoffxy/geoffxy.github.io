Hi there! My name is Geoffrey and I'm a [Computer Science](http://web.cs.toronto.edu) master's student at the
[University of Toronto](https://www.utoronto.ca). Previously I was a
[Software Engineering](https://uwaterloo.ca/software-engineering/) student at the
[University of Waterloo](https://uwaterloo.ca/) and graduated in 2018.

As a part of my undergraduate studies, I have completed six internships. I first spent the summer of 2014 at
[Milq](https://milq.com) as a Full Stack Software Engineering Intern working on various
[projects](https://milq.com/milq-blog/follow-and-discover-your-interests-29) throughout the site.

In the winter of 2015, I had the opportunity to intern at [Coursera](https://coursera.org) on the University
Product team [building](https://building.coursera.org/blog/2015/05/01/stepping-up-to-the-challenge) authoring
interfaces for instructors. I returned to Coursera in the fall of 2015, this time focusing on the back-end stack to
help [scale content production](https://building.coursera.org/blog/2016/01/10/scaling-content-production).

During the summer of 2016, I interned at [Apple](https://www.apple.com) on its Cassandra infrastructure team where I
learned quite a bit about distributed systems and databases. There I
[contributed several patches](https://github.com/apache/cassandra/commits?author=geoffxy) to Apache Cassandra to
improve how timeouts are respected during a query and to improve error messaging by enabling the passing back of error
codes from all replica nodes participating in a query.

In the winter of 2017, I interned at [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com) on the
[Nuclide](https://nuclide.io) team, where I primarily worked on Java language support in an effort to bring a
world-class Java development experience to Nuclide.

I returned to Facebook in the fall of 2017 and interned on the Async Tier team---an infrastructure team responsible for
Facebook's asynchronous job execution platform. I helped develop a new queuing service and added support for
rescheduling failed jobs.

In my spare time I enjoy taking photos, blogging, listening to music, and catching up on my favourite television shows.

You can find me on Twitter as [@GeoffCodes](https://twitter.com/GeoffCodes) and on GitHub as
[@geoffxy](https://github.com/geoffxy).
